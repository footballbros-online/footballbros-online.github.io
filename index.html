    Gameplay Mechanics

    Movement and Controls

    Control your player using the Arrow Keys or W A S D for full movement across the field:

    • W / Up Arrow – Move forward
    • S / Down Arrow – Move backward
    • A / Left Arrow – Move left
    • D / Right Arrow – Move right
    • Spacebar – Shoot / Kick
    • Shift – Dash / Sprint
    • Ctrl / Enter – Tackle / Steal

    Quick reactions are essential. Using these controls efficiently helps you stay in position, win possession, and create scoring chances.

    Strategy and Match Flow

    Matches are short, but positioning and timing make a big difference.

    • Attacking – Move forward quickly when the ball is free. Getting into position early increases your chances of scoring.
    • Defending – Stay near your goal, read your opponent’s movement, and use tackles at the right moment to regain control.
